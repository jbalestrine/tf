Open Google services in a new tab. Sign-in and account permissions are handled by Google.

Want your mail and calendar inside MasterChief? Use Mail & Calendar - it connects Gmail, Yahoo and other IMAP accounts with app passwords and shows calendars from ICS links. No Google Cloud project needed.
These are Google app shortcuts, not API connections inside MasterChief. Reading meetings, email, or translating app content from this site requires a Google Cloud OAuth client and the appropriate API scopes. No Google credentials or OAuth client have been configured in this project.
