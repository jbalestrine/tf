Accounts

Add an account

Use an app password, not your normal password. Gmail: Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords. Yahoo: Account Security → Generate app password. Credentials are stored only in this app's local data/mailcal_accounts.json and the mailbox is opened read-only.

Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → Secret address in iCal format. Yahoo/others: any calendar's published ICS link.
